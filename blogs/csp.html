Understanding Content Security Policy

A practical look at CSP, browser behaviour and the security assumptions behind modern web applications.

Content Security Policy is one of the browser's mechanisms for controlling which resources a page is allowed to load or execute.

Why CSP Matters

A well-designed policy can reduce the impact of certain cross-site scripting scenarios by restricting where executable content may originate.

Think in Directives

Rather than treating CSP as one large rule, think about it as a collection of resource-specific policies.

Content-Security-Policy:
default-src 'self';
script-src 'self';
img-src 'self' https://images.example.com;

Final Thought

The best CSP is one that reflects the application's real resource requirements instead of being copied blindly from a generic configuration.
