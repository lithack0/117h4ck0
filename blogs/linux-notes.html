Notes From Learning Linux

Small discoveries, useful commands and mental models that made Linux easier to understand.

Linux became much easier once I stopped memorising commands and started understanding what the operating system was actually doing.

The Filesystem Is a Concept

Directories such as /etc, /var, /usr and /proc have different purposes. Understanding those purposes is more useful than memorising their names.

A Useful Habit

When something behaves unexpectedly, inspect the manual page, check the exit status and look at the relevant logs.

$ command --help
$ man command
$ echo $?
$ journalctl
